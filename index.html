About Our Firm

Our Story

The law firm is co-founded by Yovana Lopez Medina and Justina E. Gonzalez, our firm is rooted in personal experience and a deep understanding of the immigration journey. Yovana’s own immigrant experience shaped her understanding of the fear, uncertainty, and courage involved in the process, while Justina’s firsthand experience witnessing the challenges her family faced gives her a personal understanding of the stress and impact of immigration matters.

Together, we approach every case with empathy, dignity, and personal attention, advocating tirelessly for our clients and the dreams they are working to build. Through transparent fees, clear communication, personalized representation, and professional systems, we strive to make the legal process more understandable and accessible while providing strong, effective advocacy.

Our goal is to be more than a law firm, to be a trusted community resource and dedicated advocate for immigrants, helping individuals and families protect their rights, remain together, and build a secure future in the United States.

Practice Areas

Legal Services

Focused representation designed to safeguard your legal status, reunite your loved ones, and defend your rights in court.

01

Family-Based Immigration

Bringing families together through immediate relative petitions, adjustment of status (green cards), spousal petitions, fiancé visas (K-1), consular processing, and unlawful presence waivers.

02

Humanitarian Visas

Dedicated legal protection for individuals fleeing violence or persecution, including U Visas (victims of qualifying crimes), T Visas (trafficking), VAWA self-petitions, Asylum, and Temporary Protected Status (TPS).

03

Removal Defense

Vigorous representation before the Immigration Court (EOIR). Defending against deportation through Cancellation of Removal, bond hearings, motions to reopen, and strategic court advocacy.
