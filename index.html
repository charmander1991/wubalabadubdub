About Our Firm

Our Story

Your Dream, Our Mission, PLLC was founded on the fundamental belief that every individual and family striving for the American Dream deserves honest, relentless, and deeply personal legal advocacy.

Navigating the United States immigration system is one of the most high-stakes journeys an individual or family can undertake. With offices located in South Oklahoma City, our team is firmly rooted in the local community while championing clients across immigration courts and USCIS offices nationwide.

We know that behind every case file, USCIS petition, or court docket is a parent, a spouse, a dreamer, or an individual fighting for safety. We treat your story with the dignity, urgency, and precision it deserves.

Practice Areas

Legal Services

Focused representation designed to safeguard your legal status, reunite your loved ones, and defend your rights in court.

01

Family-Based Immigration

Bringing families together through immediate relative petitions, adjustment of status (green cards), spousal petitions, fiancé visas (K-1), consular processing, and unlawful presence waivers.

02

Humanitarian Visas

Dedicated legal protection for individuals fleeing violence or persecution, including U Visas (victims of qualifying crimes), T Visas (trafficking), VAWA self-petitions, Asylum, and Temporary Protected Status (TPS).

03

Removal Defense

Vigorous representation before the Immigration Court (EOIR). Defending against deportation through Cancellation of Removal, bond hearings, motions to reopen, and strategic court advocacy.
